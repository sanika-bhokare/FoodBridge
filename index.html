How FoodBridge Works

Bridging surplus food to hungry souls in 4 simple steps.

01

Post Extra Food

Donors (restaurants, caterers, hostels) list available fresh food quantity, expiry time, and pickup address in under 30 seconds.

02

Instant Geo Alerts

Nearby verified shelters, orphanages, and food rescue volunteers receive instant notification alerts.

03

Safety Inspection

Volunteers conduct hygiene checks, temperature verification, and seal food in sanitized transport containers.

04

Rapid Distribution

Meals are transported directly to shelters and community kitchens before expiry, serving wholesome food to people in need.

Highest Standards

Strict Food Safety & Hygiene Standards

Food safety is our top priority. FoodBridge enforces strict safety protocols based on international food handling guidelines:

Thermal Chain Protection

Hot foods maintained above 60°C; chilled foods kept below 5°C during transport.

4-Hour Window Policy

Cooked food is redistributed and served strictly within 4 hours of preparation.

Sanitized Packaging Rules

All food must be packaged in unused, clean, food-grade containers.
